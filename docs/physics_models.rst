Physics models
##############

TORAX provides a modular framework for incorporating various physics models.
This section summarizes the presently implemented models for
:ref:`magnetic geometry <physics_geometry>`,
:ref:`plasma composition and initial/prescribed conditions <physics_plasma_composition>`,
:ref:`turbulent transport <physics_transport>`,
:ref:`neoclassical physics <physics_neoclassical>`,
:ref:`rotation <rotation_physics>`,
:ref:`edge physics <physics_edge>`,
:ref:`sources and sinks <physics_sources>`,
:ref:`fast ions <physics_fast_ions>`, and
:ref:`MHD <physics_mhd>`. Extended physics features are a
focus of the short-term development roadmap. Specific plans are summarized
following each subsection.
